# RoomScanStudio design context

RoomScanStudio is a field instrument for people who capture, inspect, and
revise rooms. Slice 5 adds a professional cross-device recovery surface; it
must make data ownership and immutable branch identity obvious without making
the existing guest workflow feel network-dependent.

## Users and intent

- Guest users keep scanning, saving, viewing, editing, exporting, and importing
  entirely offline and without an account.
- Authenticated professionals explicitly migrate selected local rooms into a
  recoverable hosted workspace and resolve stale branches themselves.
- The key feeling is calm, precise trust: the interface should explain what is
  copied, what remains local, and what cannot be silently merged.

## Visual language

- Continue the existing warm paper, dark ink, blueprint teal, and warning amber
  system in `AppPalette`; colors remain semantic and adaptive.
- Continue the existing serif headings, rounded body copy, and monospaced fact
  rails in `AppTypography`. Do not introduce Inter, Roboto, or Arial.
- Prefer field-notebook rules, compact fact rows, and restrained raised
  surfaces. Avoid gradients, neon glow, glass effects, generic dashboard card
  grids, emoji icons, and blanket entrance animation.
- One primary action per region. Keep compare/rebase/duplicate visibly distinct
  and never present an inferred geometry merge.

## Product constraints visible in the UI

- Migration is previewed and explicitly approved; the local room is never
  deleted.
- The default hosted archive contains the recoverable working set, while RGB,
  depth, confidence, and diagnostics remain local.
- Raw archive enablement requires a separate size/privacy review.
- A stale expected head preserves both immutable branches and requires Compare,
  Rebase as copy, or Duplicate locally.
- Show exact sizes, revisions, phases, and digests where they help establish
  identity. Keep local, private CloudKit backup, and professional hosted sync
  described as separate tiers.

## Responsive and accessible behavior

- Support iPhone portrait, iPad, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver reading order, 44-point
  controls, high contrast, dark appearance, and Reduce Motion.
- Use `ViewThatFits` or adaptive stacking for action groups rather than fixed
  widths. Long identifiers and localized copy must wrap without clipping.
- Deterministic screenshot fixtures are DEBUG-only, simulator-only, synthetic,
  and zero-network; they must remain unreachable in production composition.
